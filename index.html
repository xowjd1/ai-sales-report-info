AI Sales Daily Report

A personal reporting tool operated by xowjd1 for the operator's own product sales.

The tool reads product and sales information from connected platforms, and uses read-only Gmail access to retrieve Fiverr and itch.io notification emails. Validated product totals can be sent to a Discord destination selected by the operator.

Gmail access is read-only. The tool cannot send, edit, or delete email. It is not offered as a public service and does not accept other users' accounts.

A Gmail permission grant permits reading the mailbox, while this tool limits its searches to Fiverr and itch.io senders. Email notifications alone do not prove complete sales records; recurring reports remain disabled until the required records are validated.
